# 📊 2026 Mid-Year Agency Pulse Survey Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **2026 Mid-Year Agency Pulse Survey Dashboard** is a Power BI solution designed to analyze employee and stakeholder feedback collected through a mid-year agency survey. The dashboard helps leadership teams evaluate performance across key operational and partnership dimensions, identify areas of improvement, and monitor engagement across global regions and markets.

This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive view of survey responses, average scores, KPI performance, market-level insights, regional comparisons, qualitative feedback, and geographical response distribution.

---

##  Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to:

- Measure overall agency performance through survey responses.
- Track satisfaction across critical business KPIs.
- Compare performance across regions and markets.
- Identify strengths and improvement opportunities.
- Analyze qualitative comments for actionable insights.
- Support data-driven decision-making for leadership teams.

##  Dashboard Features

### 1. Survey Filters

Interactive slicers allow users to analyze responses by:

- Region
- Market

This enables focused analysis at global, regional, or market levels.

---

### 2. Executive Summary KPIs

The dashboard provides high-level metrics including:

- **Overall Responses** – Total survey responses received.
- **Average Survey Score** – Average rating across all survey questions.
- **Performance Category** – Automatically classified based on rating scale.

---

### 3. Dynamic Rating Scale

A visual performance scale translates average survey scores into business-friendly categories:

| Score Range | Category |
|------------|------------|
| 1.0 - 1.5 | Below Expectations |
| 1.5 - 2.5 | Needs Improvement |
| 2.5 - 3.5 | Meets Expectations |
| 3.5 - 4.5 | Above Expectations |
| 4.5 - 5.0 | Outstanding |

This allows stakeholders to quickly understand overall survey performance.

---

### 4. KPI Performance Analysis

The **Average Score by Question KPI** section evaluates performance across multiple dimensions:

- Collaboration
- Compliance
- Partnership
- Leadership
- Satisfaction
- Solutions
- Timeliness
- Strategic Thinking
- Change Management
- Value Creation

#### Key Benefits

✅ Identify highest-rated strengths

✅ Highlight low-performing areas

✅ Prioritize improvement initiatives

✅ Monitor organizational capability performance

---

### 5. Market Performance Analysis

The **Average Score by Market** visual compares survey performance across different markets.

#### Insights Supported

- Top-performing markets
- Underperforming markets
- Benchmark comparisons
- Regional performance variations
- Improvement opportunities by geography

---

### 6. Regional Performance Scorecards

Performance is summarized across major global regions:

- Enterprise
- AP & ANZ
- Europe
- EEMEA
- LAC
- United States
- Canada

Each regional scorecard displays:

- Average Score
- Performance Status
- Regional Ranking

This enables quick executive-level performance reviews.

---

### 7. Qualitative Feedback Analysis

The dashboard includes open-text survey comments to complement quantitative insights.

#### Benefits

- Understand reasons behind ratings
- Capture stakeholder sentiment
- Surface recurring themes
- Identify opportunities for improvement
- Support leadership action planning

---

### 8. Geographic Response Distribution

An interactive world map visual displays survey participation by location.

#### Key Benefits

- View response distribution globally
- Track participation by region
- Identify engagement gaps
- Support future survey planning
