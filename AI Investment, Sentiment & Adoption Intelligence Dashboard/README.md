#  AI Adoption & Sentiment Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI Adoption & Sentiment Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution designed to monitor artificial intelligence adoption trends, investment patterns, business outcomes, and organizational sentiment across industries and regions.

This dashboard enables decision-makers to evaluate AI implementation success, measure adoption rates, assess return on investment (ROI), understand stakeholder sentiment, and compare AI maturity levels across sectors and geographies.

---

##  Business Objective

The primary goal of this dashboard is to provide a comprehensive view of AI adoption performance by:

- Tracking AI adoption rates across organizations.
- Measuring estimated ROI from AI investments.
- Analyzing sentiment towards AI initiatives.
- Comparing AI adoption across industries and regions.
- Monitoring adoption growth trends over time.
- Supporting strategic AI investment decisions.

---

##  Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is divided into several analytical sections that provide insights into AI adoption performance, sentiment trends, and business impact.

---

##  Executive KPI Summary

The top section provides a high-level overview of key AI adoption metrics.

### Key KPIs

| Metric | Description |
|----------|------------|
| Avg. Adoption Rate | Average AI adoption across surveyed organizations |
| Annual Budget (USD) | Average annual investment allocated to AI initiatives |
| Organizations Surveyed | Total number of surveyed organizations |
| Share Percentage | Market share or adoption share indicator |
| Total ROI Estimated | Estimated return generated through AI investments |

These metrics help executives quickly understand the state of AI adoption and investment performance.
