## 📌 Project Overview

The Dance Diversity Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to explore the richness of India's cultural heritage through traditional dance forms, festival participation, and audience engagement across states.

The dashboard provides insights into dance popularity, participation trends, state-wise representation, festival associations, and audience engagement patterns, enabling users to better understand the diversity and reach of traditional Indian dance forms.

---

##  Business Objective

The primary objectives of this dashboard are to:

- Analyze participation levels across traditional dance forms.
- Identify states with the highest cultural engagement.
- Explore relationships between dance performances and festivals.
- Track participation trends over time.
- Understand regional diversity in cultural activities.
- Support cultural research and awareness initiatives.

##  Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive KPI Cards

The dashboard provides key cultural participation metrics:

- Total Dance Forms
- Average Participation Percentage
- State with Highest Participation Rate
- Festival Participation Count

These KPIs provide a quick summary of cultural engagement across the dataset.

---

### 2. Dance Form Participation Analysis

The dashboard ranks dance forms based on average participation rates.

#### Key Insights

- Most popular dance forms
- Audience preference patterns
- Comparative participation analysis
- High-engagement cultural performances

Examples include:

- Kathakali
- Manipuri
- Langvir Nritya
- Ghoomar
- Dekhnni

---

### 3. State-wise Cultural Representation

A treemap visualization highlights the distribution of dance participation across Indian states.

States represented include:

- West Bengal
- Tamil Nadu
- Rajasthan
- Kerala
- Gujarat
- Karnataka
- Punjab
- Telangana
- Uttar Pradesh
- Nagaland
- Mizoram
- Assam

#### Benefits

- Compare participation levels across states
- Identify culturally active regions
- Visualize state diversity contribution

---

### 4. Festival and Dance Relationship Analysis

The dashboard showcases the relationship between traditional festivals and dance performances.

Features include:

- Festival selection filter
- Dance popularity by festival
- Festival participation counts
- Audience engagement tracking

This helps identify which cultural celebrations drive maximum dance participation.

---

### 5. Participation Trend Analysis

A trend chart tracks audience participation over time.

#### Insights Supported

- Historical participation growth
- Seasonal cultural trends
- Festival impact on engagement
- Long-term audience behavior

---

### 6. Dance Distribution Analysis

Interactive donut and pie charts provide a breakdown of:

- Dance performances by category
- Festival-wise dance distribution
- Participation share analysis

These visuals make it easy to compare dance representation across categories.

---

### 7. Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically analyze data using:

- Festival Filter
- Dance Selection Filter

This enables focused exploration of specific dances or cultural events.

---

##  Key Business Questions Answered

The dashboard helps answer:

- Which dance forms have the highest participation?
- Which Indian states contribute most to cultural participation?
- What is the average participation rate across dance forms?
- Which festivals showcase the most dance performances?
